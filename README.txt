Burrow: rabbit hole map + weather tint + ambient music

Install:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped "burrow" folder.
4. Click the Burrow icon, type your city, and pick a Music option.

Music:
- "Original ambient music" is generated on the fly (two moods to choose from).
- "My own tracks": use "Add or remove my tracks" in the popup to add audio files you own.

After editing any file, click the reload icon on the extension card
